HomeTerms of Service

What we access

  • user.info.basic: the authorized account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar), only to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to send videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

What we store

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, to avoid signing in on every upload. No database or server stores TikTok data. We do not collect data from viewers or from any other TikTok users.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, do not use it for advertising, and do not read messages, followers, comments or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner deletes them or revokes access in TikTok (Settings › Security › Apps and services permissions). Revoking access immediately stops the tool from working on that account.
