Caso Aberto Publisher is an internal tool used by the Caso Aberto channels to upload their own short documentary videos about well-documented, historical criminal cases to their own TikTok accounts.

How it works

  1. The channel owner produces a video (original narration, original music, licensed or public-domain images).
  2. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the tool for their own account.
  3. The tool sends the video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API). The owner reviews it in the TikTok app, adds the caption and the AI-generated content label, and publishes it.

The tool is used only by the channel owner on their own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access other users' data.
